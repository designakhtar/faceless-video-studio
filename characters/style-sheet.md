# Character & Style Sheet — Akhtar Hossain channel

Shared by every video on this channel. The three VEO blocks at the end are built into every Veo prompt; the others are notes for people.

## Hero
- Name: Pixo
- Role: Akhtar's creative mascot — a tiny 3D robot-artist who designs, edits and animates. Stands in for Akhtar (faceless channel).

## CHARACTER BLOCK
Pixo, a small friendly robot-artist about 60 centimeters tall, rounded glossy white body, large round head with a black glass visor face showing two soft cyan (#4FD1FF) oval eyes, short stubby arms holding a glowing orange stylus pen, a bright orange (#FF8A3D) scarf, a short antenna ending in a tiny glowing paintbrush tip, smooth appealing rounded proportions, no mouth, no text or logos on the body.

## STYLE BLOCK
Stylized 3D animated feature-film look, soft global illumination, warm key light from the left with cool cyan rim light, gentle subsurface scattering, shallow depth of field, 35mm lens, rich but soft palette of deep navy (#141833), violet (#3A2C6B), warm orange (#FF8A3D) and cyan (#4FD1FF), clean uncluttered sets, smooth subtle motion, cinematic 16:9.

## AVOID
Avoid: readable text, letters, captions, subtitles, logos, watermarks, realistic humans, photoreal style, extra robots that look like Pixo, distorted hands, flicker, sudden style changes.

## Supporting cast (keep simple to stay consistent)
- Townspeople: small stylized rounded 3D figures with simple faces, varied pastel clothing, seen mostly from a distance.
- The shop: a small cozy corner shop with a round awning in violet and orange, large front window.
