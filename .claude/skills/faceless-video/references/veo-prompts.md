# Writing Veo shots (lessons from creative-promo)

- One action per shot, and say how it ENDS. "Taps the box and it transforms" left the box in place. Write
  "the box is gone; in its place a glowing bottle floats".
- Count things: "exactly three orbs". Say which hand holds what: "one pen in its right hand".
- State position plainly: "standing on top of the awning". Say the light: "windows dark, no neon, shop unlit".
- Never put in a prompt: hex codes, names, sizes, brand words, "logo", "thumbnail", UI words. They can appear as
  text. For screens and posters write "abstract colour shapes only".
- Keep objects away from the hero's face.
- Same place in two shots: repeat the setting sentence exactly.
- Vary shot sizes; never more than two of the same in a row.
- Invent original characters; do not imitate a studio's characters.
- `characters/pixo-ref.png` is the look to match. Attaching it as an Ingredient in Flow may help consistency, but
  that is untested. Check Flow's displayed price again after attaching.

The cleaned VEO blocks have passed the linter but have not yet been used in a paid generation: judge them on the
test shot. A new hero: copy `style-sheet-template.md`, keeping the three VEO blocks.
