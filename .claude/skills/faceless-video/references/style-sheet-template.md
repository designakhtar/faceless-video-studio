# Character & Style Sheet — template

Copy to `characters/style-sheet.md` and fill in. The three VEO blocks are
built into every Veo prompt by `studio.py prompts`; the other blocks are notes for people. Keep each block under ~70 words — long blocks dilute the shot action.

## Hero
- Name: Pip
- Role: curious guide who explores each topic

## CHARACTER BLOCK
Pip, a small friendly robot-explorer about 1 meter tall, rounded white ceramic body,
large round head with a glossy black visor face showing two soft cyan (#4FD1FF) oval eyes,
short stubby arms, orange (#FF8A3D) scarf, small antenna with a glowing cyan tip,
smooth rounded proportions, no mouth, no text or logos on the body.

## STYLE BLOCK
Stylized 3D animated feature-film look, soft global illumination, warm key light from the left
with cool rim light, gentle subsurface scattering, shallow depth of field, 35mm lens,
saturated but soft palette of navy (#1B1F3A), purple (#3A2C6B), warm orange (#FFB347) and cyan (#4FD1FF),
clean uncluttered sets, smooth subtle motion, cinematic 16:9.

## AVOID (negative list — add to every prompt as "Avoid:")
text, captions, subtitles, logos, watermarks, extra characters that look like the hero,
realistic humans, photoreal style, distorted hands, flicker, sudden style changes.
